Digital Safety and Awareness

Digital safety means protecting our personal information, online accounts, devices, and activities while using the internet. Everyone should have access to clear and accessible online safety information.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords that are difficult to guess. Use different passwords for different accounts. A trusted password manager may help you manage them safely.

2. Be Careful with Suspicious Links

Avoid clicking unexpected links or opening unknown attachments. Check whether the sender and website are trustworthy before sharing any information.

3. Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), or banking details with unknown people. Be careful about sharing private information online.

4. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds another verification step when signing in to an account. Use it when available.

5. Use Trusted Websites and Applications

Check website addresses carefully and download applications from official sources. Avoid suspicious downloads.

6. What Is Phishing?

Phishing is an online scam in which someone pretends to be a trustworthy person or organisation to trick people into sharing private information or clicking unsafe links.

Example of a Phishing Message

You receive a message claiming that your bank account will be blocked unless you immediately click a link and share an OTP. This could be a scam.

How to Stay Safe

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Verify messages through official channels.
  • Report suspicious messages when appropriate.

7. Accessible Online Services

Websites should provide clear instructions, readable text, useful image descriptions, captions, keyboard navigation, and understandable error messages wherever appropriate.

Online Safety Checklist

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Check suspicious links before opening them.
  • Protect private information.
  • Review account privacy settings.
  • Know how to report online scams.
