Awareness Activities and Survey

This page focuses on spreading awareness about digital accessibility, assistive technologies, and safe internet use for persons with disabilities.

Purpose of the Awareness Program

The purpose of this program is to provide useful information about accessible technology and encourage the safe and independent use of digital services. It also aims to increase understanding of the digital barriers that some persons with disabilities experience.

Awareness Activities

1. Digital Accessibility Awareness

Explain why accessible websites and applications are important. Introduce features such as screen readers, magnification, captions, and voice typing.

2. Online Safety Awareness

Share tips about strong passwords, suspicious links, online scams, privacy, and protecting personal information.

3. Sharing Educational Resources

Share reliable resources that explain accessibility features and help people learn how to use digital technology safely.

Awareness Survey

A survey can help identify awareness of digital accessibility, the use of assistive technology, and difficulties experienced while using websites and online services.

Sample Survey Questions

  1. Have you heard about digital accessibility?
  2. Do you know about screen readers or magnification tools?
  3. Have you used accessibility features on a phone or computer?
  4. Do you face difficulties while using websites or applications?
  5. Are online forms and educational websites easy for you to use?
  6. Do you know how to identify suspicious links and online scams?
  7. Would you like to learn more about assistive technology?
  8. What improvements would make digital services easier to use?
