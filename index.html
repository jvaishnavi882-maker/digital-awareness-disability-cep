Digital Awareness Program for Persons with Disabilities

Making Digital Technology Accessible for Everyone

Welcome to our digital awareness website! This project aims to spread awareness about digital accessibility, assistive technology, and safe internet use for persons with disabilities.

About Our Project

Digital technology helps us communicate, learn, and access information. However, some persons with disabilities face difficulties while using websites, mobile applications, and online services. Our project provides useful information to help make digital technology more accessible, safe, and inclusive.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote digital awareness and encourage equal opportunities for everyone in the digital world.
