About the Project

Digital Awareness Program for Persons with Disabilities

Introduction

Digital awareness means having the knowledge and skills needed to use digital devices, websites, applications, and internet services. Technology plays an important role in education, communication, banking, and everyday life.

However, persons with disabilities may face difficulties when digital services are not designed to meet different accessibility needs. This project aims to spread awareness about accessible technology and safe internet use.

Need for the Project

  • To encourage equal access to digital information.
  • To promote accessible online education.
  • To introduce useful assistive technologies.
  • To encourage safe and responsible internet use.
  • To increase awareness about digital inclusion.

Objectives

  • Explain the importance of digital accessibility.
  • Identify common digital barriers.
  • Introduce screen readers, captions, and magnification tools.
  • Share online safety tips and educational resources.
  • Encourage digital independence and inclusion.

Scope of the Project

This project covers digital literacy, accessibility features, assistive technology, internet safety, awareness activities, and useful online resources.

Target Audience

The website is intended for persons with disabilities, students, teachers, family members, caregivers, and community volunteers.

Expected Outcomes

The project aims to improve awareness of digital accessibility, introduce helpful technology tools, and promote safer and more inclusive use of digital services.
